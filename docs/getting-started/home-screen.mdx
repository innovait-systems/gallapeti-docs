# Find your way around

**When you'd use this:** to get to know the app after setting up your shop.

## The Home screen

<Screenshot src="getting-started/home.webp" alt="Home: greeting, today's sales card, profit and bills, and the four big buttons." />

- **Today's sales** — the total so far, with how it compares to yesterday at the same time.
- **Today's profit** and **Bills today** — tap them for the full [report](../sales-reports/reports.mdx).
  Profit shows only after you add cost prices to items.
- **Big buttons** — **New Sale**, **Items**, **Sales** and **Reports**.
- **Recent bills** — your last three bills; tap one to open it.

## The tabs at the bottom

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Today at a glance |
| **New Sale** | Make a bill — see [Make a bill](../billing/new-sale.mdx) |
| **Sales** | All past bills — see [Past bills](../sales-reports/sales-history.mdx) |
| **Items** | What you sell — see [Add an item](../items/add-item.mdx) |
| **Settings** | Shop details, printer, backup, feedback |

:::tip[Busy counter?]
Turn on **Settings → Counter → Open on New Sale** so the app opens straight on billing. See
[Counter settings](../settings/counter-settings.mdx).
:::
