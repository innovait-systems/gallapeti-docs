# Set up your shop

**When you'd use this:** the first time you open Galla Peti. It takes about two minutes, and you
can change everything later.

## Steps

1. On the welcome screen, tap **Get Started**.

   <Screenshot src="getting-started/welcome.webp" alt="Welcome screen with the Galla Peti logo, Get Started and Restore from backup." />

2. **Choose what you sell:** Kirana / Grocery; Restaurant / Bakery / Ice cream; Clothing & Fashion;
   Hardware, Paints & Electricals; Mobile & Electronics; Fashion Jewellery; Stationery, Gifts &
   Fancy; Salon & Services; Crackers; or **Other shop**. Galla Peti sets units, GST and billing style
   to match — for example kg for loose grocery items, litres and metres for paints and pipes, or
   token numbers for food counters. Tap **Continue**.

   Chose **Other shop**? On the next screen you can type what you sell (e.g. *Pet shop*), pick your
   usual GST rate and whether your prices include GST.

3. **Enter your shop details.** Only the **shop name** is required.
   - **Logo** — tap the image box to pick your logo. It prints at the top of every bill.
   - **Mobile number** and **address** — printed on bills.
   - **GSTIN** *(optional)* — if you're GST registered. Galla Peti checks it for typos and fills in
     your state.
   - **UPI ID** *(optional)* — customers can scan a QR with the amount already filled in.

   <Screenshot src="getting-started/shop-details.webp" alt="Shop details: logo, shop name, mobile number, address, GSTIN and UPI ID." />

4. Keep **Add sample items** on if you want a few example items to try a bill right away. You can
   edit or delete them later.

5. Tap **Start billing**. You land on the Home screen.

## Tips

- Moving from another phone? Tap **Restore from backup** on the welcome screen instead — see
  [Backup & restore](../settings/backup-restore.mdx).
- Setting up an extra counter phone for a shop that already uses Galla Peti Pro? Tap **Join a main
  phone (extra counter)** and scan the QR code on the owner's phone — see
  [Bill on more than one phone](../settings/multiple-phones.mdx).
- Change anything later in **Settings → Shop details** — see [Shop details](../settings/shop-details.mdx).
