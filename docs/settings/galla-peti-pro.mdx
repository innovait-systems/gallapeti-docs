# Growth and Pro plans

**Galla Peti is free forever** for unlimited bills, GST, printing, UPI QR, WhatsApp bills, bills in
your language, Excel import and reports. Two optional plans add more:

- **Growth** is for a busy shop on one phone: bill faster and look more professional.
- **Pro** is for shops with staff and more than one counter. It includes everything in Growth.

## What Growth adds

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Fast entry** | Scan a barcode (Bluetooth / USB scanner) or type an item no. and press **Enter**. The item goes straight into the bill, ready for the next scan. See [Make a bill](../billing/new-sale.mdx). |
| **Barcode stickers & weighed packets** | Give loose items their own barcode and print stickers on your receipt printer. Weighed packets carry the weight in the barcode, so one scan bills it. See [Barcode stickers](../items/barcode-stickers.mdx). |
| **Bill to a business** | The buyer's name, address and **GSTIN** on the bill, with IGST worked out for buyers in another state. See [Review the bill](../billing/review-bill.mdx). |
| **A4 GST invoice** | A full-page invoice PDF with HSN, taxable value, GST and amount in words, to share or print on any printer. See [Send the bill](../billing/send-bill.mdx). |
| **Quotes** | Save a bill as a quotation, send it, and turn it into a bill later. See [Quotes](../billing/quotes.mdx). |
| **More reports and GST** | Item-wise sales and profit for any month, quarter or year, items that didn't sell, a GST summary and an Excel file in the GSTR-1 layout. See [More reports and GST](../sales-reports/more-reports.mdx). |
| **Share your price list** | Send customers your prices and offers on WhatsApp, or as an A4 PDF: all items, a few categories or the ones you pick. Open it from **Items → Price list**. |
| **Close the day** | Count the cash drawer at closing time. Galla Peti shows short or excess, and prints a day-end summary. See [Close the day](../sales-reports/day-end-closing.mdx). |
| **A4 credit note** | A full-page GST credit note PDF for returns (returns themselves are free). See [Returns](../billing/returns.mdx). |
| **Longer backup history** | Keep automatic daily backups for 1, 2 or 3 months instead of 1 week. See [Backup & restore](backup-restore.mdx). |
| **No branding line** | Your bills don't show *Billed with Galla Peti · Powered by Innovait Systems*. |

## What Pro adds

Everything in Growth, plus:

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Staff PINs** | Each person signs in with their own PIN, bills show who made them, and you choose who can give discounts, take returns, void bills or see profit. See [Staff PINs](staff-pins.mdx). |
| **Bill on more than one phone** | Up to 2 counter phones bill on their own, and all sales, returns and closings come together on the owner's phone. They sync in the shop, with no internet needed. See [Bill on more than one phone](multiple-phones.mdx). |

Buttons for features you don't have yet look greyed out, with a gold **🔒 GROWTH** or **🔒 PRO**
badge. Tap one to see what it does and the plans. If staff PINs are on and a staff member taps it,
they're asked to check with the owner instead.

**Coming later:** customer credit (khata), expenses, low-stock alerts and more.
**Galla Peti Business** (planned) adds cloud sync from anywhere, unlimited phones and a computer version.

## Try Pro free for 14 days

Every shop can try all of Pro free for **14 days**: no payment, no card, nothing to cancel.

1. Open **Settings → Plan → Try Pro free** (or tap any 🔒 button).
2. Tap **Try Pro free for 14 days**. Pro turns on straight away.

**Settings → Plan** shows how many days are left, and Home reminds you in the last few days. When the
trial ends, the paid features simply turn off. Your bills, items, quotes and settings all stay; you
just can't use the locked buttons until you choose a plan. Billing, GST and printing are never affected.

The trial is once per shop. It's saved in your [backup](backup-restore.mdx), so restoring a backup on
another phone continues the same trial rather than starting a new one.

## Subscribe

1. Open **Settings → Plan**.
2. Pick **Growth** or **Pro** at the top, then a plan:

   | | Monthly | Quarterly | Yearly |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | **Growth** | ₹119 | ₹299 | **₹999** (₹83 a month) |
   | **Pro** | ₹199 | ₹499 | **₹1,799** (₹150 a month) |

   The app always shows Google Play's current prices.
3. Tap **Get Growth** or **Get Pro** and pay with Google Play: UPI, card or Play balance.

Your plan switches on straight away. No account or sign-up is needed; Google Play keeps the record of
your subscription. You can subscribe any time, including during the free trial.

### Upgrade from Growth to Pro

**Settings → Plan → Upgrade to Pro**. Pro starts straight away. Google Play gives you credit for the
unused part of your Growth plan, so you don't pay twice.

### Works offline, with a check now and then

Galla Peti confirms your subscription with Google Play about once a day when the phone is online. Your
plan keeps working **without internet for at least 7 days** after the last check (longer if you paid for
a longer period). Only the purchase reference is checked, never your bills, items or customers.

| Message on the plans screen | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Your plan is paused — connect to the internet** | Turn on mobile data or Wi-Fi, open **Settings → Plan** and tap **Check again**. |
| **This copy isn't from Google Play** | Paid plans work only in Galla Peti installed from the Play Store. Install it from there; your subscription carries over. |
| **No active subscription found** | Google Play says it ended or was refunded. Tap **Check again**, or write to us if that's wrong. |

Billing, GST and printing keep working in every case.

## New phone? Restore your plan

Sign in to the **same Google account** on the new phone, install Galla Peti, open
**Settings → Plan** and tap **Restore purchase**. Your bills and items come across with a
[backup](backup-restore.mdx).

## Cancel or change your plan

**Settings → Plan → Manage subscription** opens Google Play. You can cancel any time. Your plan keeps
working until the end of the period you've paid for, then your shop goes back to the free plan, and
nothing is deleted. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.
