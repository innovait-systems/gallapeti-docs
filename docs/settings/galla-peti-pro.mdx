# Galla Peti Pro

**Galla Peti is free forever** for unlimited bills, GST, printing, UPI QR, WhatsApp bills, Excel
import and reports. **Pro** is an optional subscription for shops that want to bill faster and look
more professional.

## What Pro adds

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Fast entry** | Scan a barcode (Bluetooth / USB scanner) or type an item no. and press **Enter** — the item goes straight into the bill, ready for the next scan. See [Make a bill](../billing/new-sale.mdx). |
| **Bill to a business** | The buyer's name, address and **GSTIN** on the bill, with IGST worked out for buyers in another state. See [Review the bill](../billing/review-bill.mdx). |
| **A4 GST invoice** | A full-page invoice PDF with HSN, taxable value, GST and amount in words — to share or print on any printer. See [Send the bill](../billing/send-bill.mdx). |
| **Quotes** | Save a bill as a quotation, send it, and turn it into a bill later. See [Quotes](../billing/quotes.mdx). |
| **More reports and GST** | Item-wise sales and profit for any month, quarter or year, items that didn't sell, a GST summary and an Excel file in the GSTR-1 layout. See [More reports and GST](../sales-reports/more-reports.mdx). |
| **Share your price list** | Send customers your prices and offers on WhatsApp, or as an A4 PDF — all items, a few categories or the ones you pick. Open it from **Items → Price list**. |
| **Close the day** | Count the cash drawer at closing time — Galla Peti shows short or excess, and prints a day-end summary. See [Close the day](../sales-reports/day-end-closing.mdx). |
| **Staff PINs** | Each person signs in with their own PIN, bills show who made them, and you choose who can give discounts, take returns, void bills or see profit. See [Staff PINs](staff-pins.mdx). |
| **A4 credit note** | A full-page GST credit note PDF for returns (returns themselves are free). See [Returns](../billing/returns.mdx). |
| **Longer backup history** | Keep automatic daily backups for 1, 2 or 3 months instead of 1 week. See [Backup & restore](backup-restore.mdx). |
| **No branding line** | Your bills don't show *Billed with Galla Peti · Powered by Innovait Systems*. |

Until you have Pro, these buttons look greyed out with a gold **🔒 PRO** badge. Tap one to see what it does and the Pro plans. If staff PINs were on and a staff member taps it, they're asked to check with the owner instead.

**Coming later in Pro:** customer credit (khata), expenses, low-stock alerts and more.

## Subscribe

1. Open **Settings → Plan → See Pro**.
2. Choose a plan:

   | Plan | Price | Works out to |
   |---|---|---|
   | **Yearly** | ₹999 / year | ₹83 a month — save 30% |
   | **Quarterly** | ₹299 / 3 months | ₹100 a month — save 16% |
   | **Monthly** | ₹119 / month | |

   The app shows Google Play's current prices. If there's a free trial or a launch price, it's shown on the
   plan.
3. Tap **Subscribe** (or **Start free trial**) and pay with Google Play — UPI, card or Play balance.

Pro switches on straight away and works **without internet**. No account or sign-up is needed;
Google Play keeps the record of your subscription.

## New phone? Restore Pro

Sign in to the **same Google account** on the new phone, install Galla Peti, open
**Settings → Plan → See Pro** and tap **Restore purchase**. Your bills and items come across with a
[backup](backup-restore.mdx).

## Cancel or change your plan

**Settings → Plan → Manage Pro → Manage subscription** opens Google Play. You can cancel any time;
Pro keeps working until the end of the period you've paid for, then your shop goes back to the free
plan — nothing is deleted. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.
