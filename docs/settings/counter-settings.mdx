# Counter settings and feedback

## Open on New Sale

**Settings → Counter → Open on New Sale.** From the next time you open the app it starts on the
**New Sale** screen instead of Home — best for busy counters.

## Send feedback

**Settings → Help → Send feedback** opens your email app addressed to
**gallapeti@innovait-systems.com**, with your app and phone version filled in. Describe what
happened, add a screenshot if you can, and send.

## Free and Pro

Galla Peti is **free forever** for unlimited bills, GST, printing, WhatsApp bills, Excel import and
reports. Free bills end with a small *Billed with Galla Peti · Powered by Innovait Systems* line.
**Galla Peti Pro** (coming soon) adds cloud backup, multiple phones, staff logins and removes the line.
