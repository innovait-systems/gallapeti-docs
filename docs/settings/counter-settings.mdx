# Counter settings, updates and feedback

## Open on New Sale

**Settings → Counter → Open on New Sale.** From the next time you open the app it starts on the
**New Sale** screen instead of Home — best for busy counters.

## Token numbers

For food counters and parlours that call out orders. **Settings → Counter → Token numbers.**

- Every bill gets a **token number**, printed big near the top and shown on screen after payment.
- Tokens start again from **1 every day**.
- Turn on **Extra token slip** to also print a small second slip with just the token and the items —
  for the counter or kitchen. It prints after payment, not when you reprint a bill later.

Shops set up as **Restaurant / Bakery / Ice cream** have token numbers on from the start.

## Updates

When a new version is on Google Play, the **Home** screen shows **Update available**. Tap
**Update**; it downloads in the background while you keep billing. Then tap **Restart** — it takes
a few seconds and your data stays.

To check yourself, or to see which version you have: **Settings → Help → Check for updates**.

## Send feedback

**Settings → Help → Send feedback** opens your email app addressed to
**gallapeti@innovait-systems.com**, with your app and phone version filled in. Describe what
happened, add a screenshot if you can, and send.

## Free and Pro

Galla Peti is **free forever** for unlimited bills, GST, printing, WhatsApp bills, Excel import and
reports. Free bills end with a small *Billed with Galla Peti · Powered by Innovait Systems* line.
**[Galla Peti Pro](galla-peti-pro.mdx)** adds fast scanner billing, B2B invoices, A4 invoices and
quotes, and removes the line. **Galla Peti Business** (later) adds cloud backup, multiple phones and staff logins.
