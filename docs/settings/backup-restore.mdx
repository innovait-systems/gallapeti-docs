# Backup & restore

Galla Peti keeps your data **on your phone**. Make a backup regularly — if the phone is lost or
reset, a backup is the only way to get your items and bills back.

## Create a backup

1. Open **Settings → Backup & restore**.
2. Tap **Create backup**.
3. Choose where to save it — **Google Drive**, email, or your own WhatsApp.

The backup includes your shop details, logo, items, all bills and your bill numbering. Keep it safe:
it contains your shop's data.

## Restore on a new phone

1. Install Galla Peti on the new phone.
2. On the welcome screen, tap **Restore from backup** and pick the backup file.
3. Check the shop name, items and bills shown, and tap **Restore**.

## Restore on the same phone

**Settings → Backup & restore → Choose backup file**, check the preview, then **Restore this backup**.

:::warning[Restore replaces data]
Restoring **replaces** the items and bills on this phone with the ones in the backup. Create a backup
of this phone first if you might need its data. Printer settings on the phone are kept.
:::

Bill numbers continue from the backup, so there are no duplicate invoice numbers.
