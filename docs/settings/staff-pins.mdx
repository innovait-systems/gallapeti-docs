# Staff PINs

**When you'd use this:** more than one person bills on the same phone — you, family members or
helpers — and you want to know who made each bill, and keep discounts, refunds and profit figures
in your hands. *Part of [Galla Peti Pro](galla-peti-pro.mdx).*

## Turn it on

1. Open **Settings → Staff & PINs**.
2. Enter **your name** and choose an **owner PIN** (4 digits — not 1111 or 1234). Enter it twice.
3. Tap **Turn on staff PINs**.
4. **Write down the recovery code** shown (like `K7PM-3QXD`) and keep it away from the counter.
   It is shown only once, and it's the only way back in if you forget the owner PIN.

## Add staff

1. **Settings → Staff & PINs → Add staff**.
2. Enter their **name** (it's printed on bills as the cashier) and a **PIN** for them. Each person
   needs a different PIN.
3. Tick what else they can do:

   | Permission | What it allows |
   |---|---|
   | **Give discounts, change prices** | Discounts and price changes on a bill |
   | **Take returns** | Make credit notes and give refunds |
   | **Void bills** | Cancel a paid bill |
   | **Add and edit items** | Prices, stock, Excel import |
   | **See reports and profit** | Sales reports, profit and cost prices |
   | **Close the day** | Count the cash drawer at closing time |

4. Tap **Add staff**.

Everyone can always make bills, put bills on hold and print. **Shop details, backup, staff and the
Pro plan are only for the owner.**

To change a name, PIN or permissions — or remove someone — tap them in the list. Bills they made keep
their name.

## Every day

- When the app opens, enter **your PIN**. Home greets you by name.
- **Changing person?** Tap the **lock** button on Home (or **Settings → Lock · switch person**) and
  the next person enters their PIN.
- The app asks for the PIN again after it has been in the background. Choose how soon in
  **Settings → Staff & PINs → Ask for the PIN again**: every time, after 5 or 30 minutes, or never.
- Bills, credit notes and day-end summaries show **who made them** (*Cashier: Priya*).

## When staff need permission

If a helper tries something they aren't allowed to do — a discount, a return, voiding a bill — the
app asks for a PIN. **You (or anyone allowed) enter your PIN on their phone** and the action goes
through once. Your PIN isn't saved for them.

After 5 wrong PINs the app waits 30 seconds before the next try.

## Forgot the owner PIN?

On the PIN screen tap **Forgot owner PIN?**, type your **recovery code**, and choose a new PIN.
You'll get a **new recovery code** — write it down; the old one no longer works.

Staff who forget their PIN: open **Settings → Staff & PINs**, tap their name and set a new PIN.

## Turn it off

**Settings → Staff & PINs → Turn off staff PINs.** Everyone's PIN is deleted and the app opens
without a PIN. Old bills keep the names they were billed by.

:::note
Staff PINs work while your shop is on Pro. If Pro ends, the app opens without a PIN; your staff list
is kept and works again when you renew. PINs are included in [backups](backup-restore.mdx), so a
restored phone asks for the same PINs.
:::
