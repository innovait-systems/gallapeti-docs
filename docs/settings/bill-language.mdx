# Bill in your language

**When you'd use this:** your customers read Tamil, Hindi or another Indian language better than
English, and you want the bill to speak to them. Free for every shop.

## Choose the language

1. Open **Settings → Bill language**.
2. Tap your language: **English, தமிழ், हिन्दी, తెలుగు, ಕನ್ನಡ, മലയാളം, मराठी, ગુજરાતી or বাংলা**.
3. **Also show English** (on by default) prints each word in both languages, like
   *மொத்தம் / TOTAL*. Turn it off for words in your language only.
4. Check the **Preview** below, or tap **Print a sample bill**.

<Screenshot src="settings/bill-language.webp" alt="Bill language: nine languages to pick from, Tamil selected, and the Also show English switch." />

The change is saved straight away and used on every phone of the shop after a
[backup and restore](backup-restore.mdx).

## What changes

The words Galla Peti prints: the bill title (*வரி விலைப்பட்டியல் / TAX INVOICE*), bill number,
cashier, column headings, discount, subtotal, total, payment, change, "You saved", the UPI line and
the thank-you line.

<Screenshot src="settings/bill-language-preview.webp" alt="A sample bill in Tamil with English alongside: title, column headings, discount, subtotal, total, cash, GST summary, UPI line and thank-you line." />

It's used everywhere a bill goes out:
- printed bills on Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and other printers,
- bills sent on **WhatsApp** and **SMS** (SMS uses your language only, to keep it short),
- PDF bills, the A4 GST invoice, quotations and credit notes.

## What stays the same

- **Item names, your shop name and address** print exactly as you typed them — in any language.
- **Numbers and dates** stay in English digits.
- **GST words** — GSTIN, HSN, CGST, SGST, IGST — and **UPI** stay in English, as on official bills.
- A footer you typed yourself in [Shop details](shop-details.mdx) prints as it is.
- On the A4 invoice, the amount in words is in English for now.

:::tip
Printing Tamil or Hindi on a thermal printer needs nothing extra — Galla Peti prints those lines as
pictures, so any 58mm or 80mm printer works.
:::

## Know a better word?

The words were written with help from translation tools and checked as carefully as we could — but you know
what customers near you say. If a word sounds wrong or too formal:

1. In **Settings → Bill language**, tap **Suggest a better word**.
2. Type the better word next to any word you want to change. Leave the rest empty.
3. Tap **Send by email**. Your email app opens with your suggestions — check it and send.

We read every suggestion and fix the words for every shop in the next update.
