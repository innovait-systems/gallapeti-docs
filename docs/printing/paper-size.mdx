# Paper size and bill look

**When you'd use this:** when you connect a printer, or if bill text wraps or gets cut off.

## Choose the paper size

Open **Settings → Printer & paper → Paper size**:

| Setting | Paper roll | Characters per line |
|---|---|---|
| **2"** | 58 mm (most common) | 32 |
| **3"** | 80 mm | 48 |
| **4"** | 112 mm | 69 |
| **Custom** | Any width 38–120 mm | Worked out for you, or set your own |

The **Preview** below shows the bill exactly as it will print. Tap **Test print** to check on paper.

<Screenshot src="printing/paper-size.webp" alt="Paper size options 2, 3, 4 inch and Custom, with the live bill preview underneath." />

## Text wraps or gets cut off?

Choose **Custom**, enter your roll width (for example 58) and adjust **Characters / line** — some
2-inch printers fit **42** characters instead of 32.

## What prints on the bill

- Your **logo**, shop name, address, phone and GSTIN — change them in
  [Shop details](../settings/shop-details.mdx).
- Items, discounts, GST, total, payment, change and **You saved**.
- A **UPI QR** on UPI bills.
- Your **bill footer** (default: *Thank you! Visit again.*).

Receipt printers print ₹ as **Rs.**; PDFs show ₹.
