# Connect a Bluetooth printer

**When you'd use this:** to print bills on a Bluetooth thermal receipt printer (most 2-inch and
3-inch printers).

## Steps

1. **Switch the printer on**, with paper loaded, and keep it near the phone.

2. In Galla Peti, open **Settings → Printer & paper**. **Bluetooth** is selected.

3. When Android asks for **Nearby devices** permission, tap **Allow**. If Bluetooth is off, tap
   **Turn on Bluetooth**.

4. Galla Peti scans automatically. Your printer appears under **Nearby** — tap **Connect**.
   If Android asks for a PIN, enter **0000** (or **1234**).

   <Screenshot alt="Printer settings: Your printers, Nearby devices with Connect buttons, and Scan for printers." />

5. When pairing finishes, Galla Peti **saves the printer and prints a test page**.

6. Check the **Paper size** (usually **2"**) — see [Paper size](paper-size.mdx).

Already paired the printer before? It's listed under **Your printers** — tap **Use**.

## After connecting

- Every bill prints automatically when you tap **Mark paid** (turn off **Print automatically after
  payment** under Options if you don't want that).
- Reprint any bill from **Sales** → open the bill → **Print**.

## Tips

- Don't know the printer's name? Hold its **FEED** button while switching it on — most printers
  print a self-test page with their Bluetooth name.
- Most Bluetooth printers connect to **one phone at a time**. For several phones on one printer,
  use a [Wi-Fi printer](wifi-printer.mdx).
