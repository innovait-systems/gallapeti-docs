# Add an item

**When you'd use this:** to list something you sell so you can add it to bills.

## Steps

1. Open **Items** and tap **Add item**.

2. **Name and category.**
   - **Item name** — as you want it on the bill, e.g. *1000 Wala* or *Cotton Shirt*.
   - **Category** *(optional)* — a group like *Sparklers*, *Mens wear* or *Earrings*. Categories
     become quick filters on the New Sale screen.

3. **Prices.** Enter **MRP**, **Selling price**, or both, and an optional **Discount %**. The box
   underneath shows exactly what the customer pays — see
   [Prices, MRP and discounts](prices-and-discounts.mdx).

   <Screenshot src="items/add-item.webp" alt="Item form: MRP 112 with a 10% discount, 'Customer pays ₹100.80 · 10% off MRP', and the cost price with your margin." />

4. **Cost price** *(optional)* — what you pay for one unit. Galla Peti uses it only to show your
   profit in reports. Customers never see it.

5. **Sold per** — pick the unit: pcs, box, kg, set, pair…

6. **GST** — pick the rate (0, 3, 5, 12, 18, 28 or 40%), choose whether the **price includes GST**,
   and add the **HSN** code if you print tax invoices.

7. **Barcode and stock** *(optional)*
   - **Barcode** — type it or tap the scan icon.
   - **Track stock** — turn on and enter how many you have; it goes down with every bill.

8. Tap **Save** — or **Save & add next** to keep the category, unit and GST for the next item.

## Tips

- **Show in favourites** puts an item first on the New Sale screen. You can also tap the ☆ star in
  the Items list.
- Adding many items? Use [Excel import](excel-import.mdx).
