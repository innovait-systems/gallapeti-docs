# Share your price list

**When you'd use this:** a customer asks "what's the rate today?", you're announcing festival
offers, or you send wholesale rates to regular buyers. *Part of
[Galla Peti Growth](../settings/galla-peti-pro.mdx).*

## Make the list

1. Open **Items** and tap **Price list**.
2. **Which items?**
   - **All** — every item. Tap category chips (like **Staples** or **Dairy**) to send only those.
   - **Favourites** — the items you marked as [favourites](edit-delete-items.mdx).
   - **Choose** — search and tick the items you want.
3. **Heading** — "Price list", or something like *Diwali offers* or *Wholesale rates*.
4. **Note** (optional) — for example *Offer valid till Sunday. Free home delivery above ₹500.*
5. Turn on or off:
   - **Show MRP and % off** — shows the MRP struck through and the saving, when your price is lower.
   - **Show item numbers** — so customers can order by number (*"#101 × 2"*).
   - **Group by category** — items under category headings.

## Send it

Check the **Preview** at the bottom — it shows exactly what the customer gets.

<Screenshot src="items/price-list-preview.webp" alt="Price list preview: shop name, date and phone, then items by category with price, MRP and % off." />

- **WhatsApp** — sends the list as a text message. Your shop name, phone and today's date are at
  the top; prices are in bold. You pick the contact or group inside WhatsApp.
- **PDF** — an A4 page with your logo, ready to share or print. The file is named after your shop
  and the heading, like *Sri-Lakshmi-Stores-Price-list-20-Nov-2026.pdf*.

For long lists, the app suggests the PDF — it's easier for customers to read.

## Tips

- Prices come straight from your items, so update a price once and the next list is right.
- Item names in Tamil, Hindi or any language show as you typed them.
