# Edit or delete items

## Edit an item

Open **Items**, tap the item, change what you need and tap **Save**. New bills use the new price;
past bills keep the price they were made with.

## Favourites

Tap the ☆ star next to an item in the **Items** list. Favourites show first on the New Sale screen
and under the **Favourites** chip.

## Low stock

If an item tracks stock, the Items list shows how many are left — in red when 5 or fewer.

## Delete one item

Open the item and tap **Delete item**.

## Delete all items

Scroll to the bottom of **Items** and tap **Delete all N items**, then confirm. A message appears
with **Undo** — tap it within a few seconds to bring everything back.

Your bills and sales history are **not** affected: past bills keep their item names and prices.
