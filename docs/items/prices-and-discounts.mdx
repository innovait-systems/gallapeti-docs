# Prices, MRP and discounts

**When you'd use this:** to understand what the customer pays for an item.

## How the price is worked out

| You enter | Customer pays | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **MRP** only | MRP | MRP ₹500 → **₹500** |
| **MRP + Selling price** | Selling price | MRP ₹1,299, SP ₹799 → **₹799** |
| **MRP + Selling price + Discount %** | Selling price less the discount | MRP ₹1,000, SP ₹800, 10% → **₹720** |
| **MRP + Discount %** | MRP less the discount | MRP ₹1,800, 80% → **₹360** |
| **Selling price** only (no MRP) | Selling price (less any discount) | SP ₹40, 25% → **₹30** |

You need at least an MRP or a selling price. The selling price can't be more than the MRP.

:::note[Discount on the selling price]
When you enter both a selling price and a Discount %, the discount comes off the **selling price** —
an extra offer on top of your price. In the example above, ₹720 is 28% below MRP, not 10%.
:::

## On the bill

The item's rate prints first and the discount on its own line, so customers see the saving:

```
1000 Wala
  1box x 1,800.00        1,800.00
  Discount 80%          -1,440.00
```

At the bottom, **You saved ₹…** shows the total saving against MRP.

## Change the discount for one bill

The item's Discount % is added to the bill automatically. To change or remove it for just this
bill, use **Discount** on the [bill review screen](../billing/review-bill.mdx).

## Cost price and profit

Add a **cost price** to an item to see profit in [reports](../sales-reports/reports.mdx). Profit is
worked out on the price **before GST** (GST isn't your income), and each bill remembers the cost at
the time of sale — changing a cost later doesn't change past profit.
