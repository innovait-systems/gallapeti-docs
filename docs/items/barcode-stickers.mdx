# Barcode stickers and weighed packets

*Part of [Galla Peti Growth](../settings/galla-peti-pro.mdx).*

Branded packets already have a barcode. For everything else — loose dal in 1 kg bags, home-made
masala, sweets — Galla Peti gives the item **its own barcode** and prints stickers on your normal
thermal receipt printer. Scan the sticker at the counter (camera or scanner) and the item goes into
the bill.

## What you need

- Your receipt printer (Bluetooth, Wi-Fi or USB, 2 inch / 58 mm or 3 inch).
- **Thermal sticker paper** that fits it — die-cut stickers (for example 50 × 25 mm) or a plain
  sticker roll you cut with scissors. Plain receipt paper works too for a test.
- No label printer is needed.

## Print stickers for an item

1. Open **Items** → the item.
2. No barcode yet? Tap **Make a barcode for this item** and **Save**. Shop barcodes start with
   **200**, so they never clash with codes on branded packets.
3. Tap **Print stickers**, choose how many, and tap **Print**.

To print for several items at once: **Items → Stickers → Add items**. Items without a barcode get
one when you print.

Choose the **sticker size** to match your roll, and whether to show the **price** and **MRP**. The
choice is remembered on this phone.

### Your price below MRP

When your price is lower than the MRP, the sticker shows both:

> MRP ~~₹100.00~~
> **Our price ₹89.00**

- **Words before your price** — type your own, like *Offer price* or *Special price*. Leave it
  empty to use *Our price* in your [bill language](../settings/bill-language.mdx) (for example
  *எங்கள் விலை* in Tamil).
- **You save** — adds *You save ₹11.00* under the price. It's off to start with; on small stickers
  it's left out when there isn't room.

When the price is the same as the MRP, only the price is printed.

### Shop name and logo

Turn on **Shop name** to print your shop's name along the top of every sticker, and **Logo** to add
your logo beside it (the same logo as on your bills — set it in **Settings → Shop details**). On
small stickers, Galla Peti leaves the logo out when there isn't room, so the barcode stays big
enough to scan. 50 × 40 mm stickers have room for both.

:::tip[Die-cut sticker rolls]
Receipt printers can't see the gaps between stickers. Before printing, pull the roll so the first
sticker starts at the tear bar; Galla Peti keeps the rest in step. For long runs, print in batches
of 20–30 and line up again.
:::

## Weighed packets (Weigh & print)

For items sold by weight that you pack ahead of time:

1. Open **Items → Stickers → Weigh & print** (or the item → **Weigh & print**) and choose the item.
2. The first time, tap **Give it item no. …** — a weighed-packet barcode needs an item no. of up to
   5 digits.
3. Weigh the packet on any scale, type the weight (for example `0.350`) and tap **Print**.

The sticker shows the weight, the price per kg and the packet price. The **weight is inside the
barcode**: scanning it adds *Toor Dal 0.35 kg* at the item's price that day.

### Packets from a label-printing scale

If your weighing scale prints its own barcode labels, set it to EAN-13 with prefix **21** + 5-digit
item no. + weight in grams (or prefix **22** + price in paise), and use the same item numbers as
in Galla Peti. Those labels then scan straight into the bill too.

## Scanning stickers with the camera

With the camera, hold one sticker inside the frame. Each sticker is added once while it stays in
view, with a short pause between items, so other stickers nearby don't get added by mistake. To
add the same item again, move the camera away and back.

## On counter phones

Counter phones can print stickers for items that already have a barcode. Making a barcode or giving
an item no. is done on the main phone.
