# Returns and credit notes

**When you'd use this:** a customer brings back some or all of what they bought — damaged, wrong
item, wrong size, or not needed — and you give the money back.

The bill itself never changes. Galla Peti makes a **credit note** for what came back, with its own
number like **CN/26-27/0001**, as GST requires.

## Take a return

1. Open **Sales** and tap the bill. (Search by bill number, customer name or phone.)
2. Tap **Return items**.
3. For each item coming back, set the quantity with **+** — or tap **All 3** to return the whole
   line. For items sold by weight (kg, litre…) type the quantity, like `0.5`.
4. Choose how you give the money back: **Cash**, **UPI** or **Card**. Pick a reason if you like
   (*Damaged*, *Wrong item*…).
5. Check the amount — it follows the bill's prices, discounts and GST — and tap **Refund ₹…**, then
   **Make credit note**.

<Screenshot src="billing/returns.webp" alt="Return items: quantity coming back for each line with All, refund by cash, UPI or card, a reason, and the refund amount." />

Stock for the returned items is added back.

## After the return

The credit note opens. From there you can:
- **Print** it on your bill printer, to hand to the customer.
- **WhatsApp** it to the customer.
- **A4 credit note PDF** — a full-page GST credit note *(part of
  [Galla Peti Pro](../settings/galla-peti-pro.mdx))*.

Find all credit notes later in **Sales → Returns**, or on the bill itself under **Returns**.

## How returns show in your sales

- **Today's sales** and [reports](../sales-reports/reports.mdx) show sales **after returns**, with the
  returns amount shown separately.
- **Cash** and **UPI** totals are reduced by refunds given in cash or UPI, so they match the drawer.
- A bill can have more than one return, until everything on it has come back. You can't return more
  than was billed.

:::note
A bill that has a return can't be [voided](void-bill.mdx). Make another return instead.
:::

## Exchange

For an exchange, take the return first (refund in cash), then make a new bill for the new item.
