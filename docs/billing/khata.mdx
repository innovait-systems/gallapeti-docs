# Khata (customer credit)

Khata is your udhaar book inside Galla Peti. Put all or part of a bill on a customer's khata, take
payments when they come in, and always see who owes what. **Khata is free.** Reminders and
statements are part of [Galla Peti Growth](../settings/galla-peti-pro.mdx).

Galla Peti never stores a balance it can't explain. The balance is always worked out from the bills,
returns and payments, so it can't drift.

## Put a bill on khata

1. Make the bill as usual and tap **Checkout**.
2. Choose **Khata**.
3. Pick the customer: type a name or phone number, or tap **New customer** to add one with just a
   name (the phone number is optional, but needed for WhatsApp).
4. If they pay part now, enter it under **Paid now** and choose cash or UPI. The rest goes on
   khata.
5. Tap **Put ₹… on khata** (or **₹… paid · ₹… on khata**).

The bill prints **On khata** and the customer's **Total due**, so they know what they owe.

<Screenshot src="billing/khata-checkout.webp" alt="Payment with Khata chosen: the customer Lakshmi Bakery owes ₹1,250, ₹100 paid now in cash and ₹233 going on khata." />

:::tip[Credit limit]
In the customer's details you can set a **limit**. If a bill would take them past it, Galla Peti
warns you. You can still go ahead. With [staff PINs](../settings/staff-pins.mdx) on, only people
allowed to **give khata past a limit** can.
:::

## See who owes you

Tap **Khata** on Home. The top shows the total due and how many people owe you. **Owe you** lists
them, biggest first. **All** shows everyone. Search by name or phone.

<Screenshot src="billing/khata-book.webp" alt="Khata book: ₹1,970 due from 3 customers, biggest first, with Add customer at the bottom." />

Tap a customer to see what they owe, since when, and every bill and payment with the balance after
each one.

<Screenshot src="billing/khata-customer.webp" alt="A customer's khata: owes ₹300 with a ₹1,000 limit, Call, Remind and Statement buttons, and the history with the balance after each entry." />

## Receive a payment

1. Open the customer and tap **Receive payment**.
2. Tap **All** or **Half**, or type the amount.
3. Choose cash, UPI (a QR with the amount appears) or card, then save.
4. Print a receipt or send it on WhatsApp.

Khata cash goes into the drawer, so [Close the day](../sales-reports/day-end-closing.mdx) counts it.

## Old khata from your notebook

When you add a customer, enter what they **already owe** from your paper khata. For a customer you
already have, open them and tap **Add old khata amount**.

## A mistake?

Payments and old amounts are never edited or deleted. Tap the entry, say why (for example
"entered twice") and tap **Reverse this entry**. It stays in the history, crossed out, and the
balance corrects itself. To undo a bill, [cancel (void) it](void-bill.mdx).

A return on a khata bill takes the amount **off the khata** by default. See
[Returns](returns.mdx).

## Reminders and statements (Growth)

- **Remind**: opens WhatsApp with a polite message showing what's due and your UPI ID. You check
  it and press Send yourself.
- **Statement**: choose this month, the last 3 months or all time. **Print** it on your receipt
  printer, **Share PDF** (an A4 page with every entry and the balance), or send the totals on
  WhatsApp.

Both use your [bill language](../settings/bill-language.mdx).

## Remove a customer

Open the customer, tap the edit button and then **Remove**. Only customers who owe nothing can be
removed. Their old bills stay.

## Counter phones

With [more than one phone](../settings/multiple-phones.mdx), counter phones can also put bills on
khata, add customers and take payments. The **main phone keeps the khata**: everything goes to it
when the counter syncs.

On a counter, a customer's balance is the main phone's balance **as of the last sync, plus what this
phone did since**. The full history and statements are on the main phone.
