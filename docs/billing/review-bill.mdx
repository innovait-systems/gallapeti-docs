# Review the bill, discounts and hold

**When you'd use this:** before taking payment — to change a quantity, give a discount, or hold
the bill while you serve someone else.

Tap **Review bill** at the bottom of the New Sale screen.

<Screenshot src="billing/review-bill.webp" alt="Current bill: items with + and − buttons, discount links, customer number and totals." />

## Change quantities

- Use **+** and **−** next to an item. Going below 1 removes it — tap **Undo** in the message at
  the bottom if that was a mistake.
- For loose items (kg, litre, metre), tap the quantity to type an exact amount.

## Give a discount

- **On one item** — tap **Discount** under the item. Choose **% off** or **₹ off**, or tap a quick
  button (5%, 10%, 20%, 50%, 80%).
- **On the whole bill** — tap **Add discount on whole bill**.

Items that have a [Discount % set](../items/prices-and-discounts.mdx) already come with their
discount; you can change or remove it here for this bill only.

## Add the customer's number *(optional)*

Enter the **mobile number** to send the bill on WhatsApp or SMS after payment, and a **name** if
you like.

## Hold a bill

Tap **Hold bill** to put it aside and start a new one. Held bills appear under **On hold** at the
top of this screen — tap **Resume** to continue. **Clear** empties the bill (with **Undo**).
