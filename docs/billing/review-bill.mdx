# Review the bill, discounts and hold

**When you'd use this:** before taking payment — to change a quantity, give a discount, or hold
the bill while you serve someone else.

Tap **Review bill** at the bottom of the New Sale screen.

<Screenshot src="billing/review-bill.webp" alt="Current bill: items with + and − buttons, discount links, customer number and totals." />

## Change quantities

- Use **+** and **−** next to an item. Going below 1 removes it — tap **Undo** in the message at
  the bottom if that was a mistake.
- For loose items (kg, litre, metre), tap the quantity to type an exact amount.

## Change an item's price for this bill

For a customer who pays a different price — a bulk buyer, or a small or credit party — tap the
item's **price** (under its name, with a pencil icon) and type the price for this bill.

- The price can go **down or up**, but **not above MRP**.
- Your saved item price doesn't change, so the next bill starts from the normal price again.
- A changed price shows in amber with the normal price next to it, e.g. *₹475 / pcs (was ₹450)*.
  To undo it, tap the price again and choose **Use normal price**.
- If the item also has a discount, the discount still applies on top of the new price.

## Give a discount

- **On one item** — tap **Discount** under the item. Choose **% off** or **₹ off**, or tap a quick
  button (5%, 10%, 20%, 50%, 80%).
- **On the whole bill** — tap **Add discount on whole bill**.

Items that have a [Discount % set](../items/prices-and-discounts.mdx) already come with their
discount; you can change or remove it here for this bill only.

## Add the customer's number *(optional)*

Enter the **mobile number** to send the bill on WhatsApp or SMS after payment, and a **name** if
you like.

## Bill to a business *(Pro)*

Selling to a business that needs a GST tax invoice? Tap **Bill to a business (GSTIN, address)**
and enter the buyer's **GSTIN** and **address**; use the name box for the business name.

- Galla Peti checks the GSTIN as you type and shows the buyer's state.
- A buyer registered in **another state** is charged **IGST** instead of CGST + SGST.
- The bill prints a **Bill to** block with the buyer's GSTIN and place of supply. For a full-page
  invoice, use **A4 invoice** after payment — see [Send the bill](send-bill.mdx).

## Save as a quote *(Pro)*

Not buying yet? Tap **Save as quote** — see [Quotes](quotes.mdx).

## Preview the bill

Tap **Preview bill** to see the bill exactly as it will print, before you take payment — for
example to check it with the customer. Nothing is saved or printed. The bill number and payment
details are added when you take payment. Tap **Charge** there to go straight to payment, or
**Back to bill** to make changes.

## Hold a bill

Tap **Hold bill** to put it aside and start a new one. Held bills appear under **On hold** at the
top of this screen — tap **Resume** to continue. **Clear** empties the bill (with **Undo**).
