# Take payment

**When you'd use this:** when the customer is ready to pay.

Tap **Charge ₹…** on the New Sale screen or the bill review screen.

## Cash

1. Choose **Cash**.
2. Type the amount the customer gave, or tap a quick button — **Exact**, or the next note
   (₹500, ₹2,000…).
3. Galla Peti shows **Change to give**.
4. Tap **Mark paid**.

## UPI

1. Choose **UPI**. A QR code appears with **the exact amount already filled in**.
2. The customer scans it with any UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm…) and pays.
3. **Check the money arrived in your UPI app**, then tap **Mark paid**.

<Screenshot src="billing/take-payment.webp" alt="UPI payment: QR code with the amount, UPI app name and dots to swipe between UPI IDs." />

:::tip[Payment failing?]
If you've added more than one UPI ID, **swipe the QR left** (or tap **›**) to show the next one.
The bill remembers which UPI ID was paid. Add UPI IDs in [Shop details](../settings/shop-details.mdx).
:::

## Card

Choose **Card**, take the payment on your card machine, then tap **Mark paid**.

## After payment

The bill is saved and gets its bill number. If a printer is connected it prints automatically.
Then you can send it — see [Send or print the bill](send-bill.mdx). Tap **New bill** to serve the
next customer.
