# Send or print the bill

**When you'd use this:** right after payment, or later from any past bill.

<Screenshot alt="After payment: customer mobile box and the WhatsApp, SMS, Print and PDF buttons." />

## WhatsApp

1. Type the customer's **10-digit mobile number** (or leave it empty to pick the contact inside
   WhatsApp).
2. Tap **WhatsApp**. WhatsApp opens the customer's chat with the bill already typed.
3. Tap **Send** in WhatsApp.

The message comes from **your own WhatsApp**, so customers see your shop's number.

## SMS

Tap **SMS**. Your SMS app opens with a short bill (shop, bill number and total) — tap **Send**.

## PDF

Tap **PDF** to share the bill as a PDF through any app — email, Drive, WhatsApp…

## Print

Tap **Print**. With a connected printer, bills also print automatically when you mark them paid —
see [Connect a Bluetooth printer](../printing/connect-bluetooth-printer.mdx).

## Send an older bill

Open **Sales**, tap the bill, and use the same buttons.
