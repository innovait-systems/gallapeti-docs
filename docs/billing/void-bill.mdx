# Cancel (void) a bill

**When you'd use this:** a bill was made by mistake, or the customer cancelled straight away.
If the customer brings goods back later, use [Returns](returns.mdx) instead — it makes a credit note
and keeps the bill as it was.

## Steps

1. Open **Sales** and tap the bill.
2. Tap **Void bill** and confirm.

The bill is marked **VOID**. It keeps its number — so your invoice numbers stay in an unbroken
series, as GST requires — but it no longer counts in sales. Stock for tracked items is added back.

:::note
A voided bill can't be un-voided. Make a new bill if needed. A bill that already has a return can't
be voided. With [staff PINs](../settings/staff-pins.mdx) on, only people allowed to void bills can do
it — others are asked for the owner's PIN.
:::
