# Cancel (void) a bill

**When you'd use this:** a bill was made by mistake, or the customer cancelled.

## Steps

1. Open **Sales** and tap the bill.
2. Tap **Void bill** and confirm.

The bill is marked **VOID**. It keeps its number — so your invoice numbers stay in an unbroken
series, as GST requires — but it no longer counts in sales. Stock for tracked items is added back.

:::note
A voided bill can't be un-voided. Make a new bill if needed.
:::
