# Quotes (estimates)

**When you'd use this:** a customer asks for prices first — a contractor, a bulk order, a gift
order — and buys later. *Quotes are part of [Galla Peti Pro](../settings/galla-peti-pro.mdx).*

## Make a quote

1. Add the items on **New Sale** as for a bill. Change prices for this customer if needed — see
   [Review the bill](review-bill.mdx).
2. On **Review bill**, add the customer's number, or their business details and GSTIN.
3. Tap **Save as quote**. It gets a number like **Q/26-27/0001** and is valid for **15 days**.

A quote doesn't take payment, doesn't change stock and doesn't count in your sales.

## Send it

On the quote screen:
- **WhatsApp** — sends the quotation as a message (enter the mobile number, or pick the contact in
  WhatsApp).
- **A4 PDF** — a full-page quotation with *Valid until*, to share or print.

## Turn it into a bill

Open the quote (**Sales → Quotes**) and tap **Make bill**. The items, prices and customer go into
**New bill** — check them, change anything, then take payment as usual. The quote is marked
**BILLED** and links to its bill. If you were in the middle of another bill, it's put on hold.

## Status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **OPEN** | Waiting for the customer. |
| **EXPIRED** | The validity date has passed. You can still make a bill — check the prices first. |
| **BILLED** | Turned into a bill. |
| **CANCELLED** | You cancelled it. It stays in the list. |
