# Make a bill

**When you'd use this:** every time a customer buys something.

## Steps

1. Open the **New Sale** tab.

   <Screenshot src="billing/new-sale.webp" alt="New Sale: search bar, category chips and the grid of items with prices." />

2. **Add items** — any of these:
   - **Tap an item** to add one. Tap again to add more. Items in the bill turn green and show
     **−  2  +** — use **−** to reduce. At 1 it becomes a bin icon that removes the item (tap **Undo** if it was a mistake).
   - **Search** — type part of the name in **Search items**.
   - **Scan** — tap the barcode icon and point the camera at the barcode. Keep scanning to add more,
     then tap **Done**.
   - **Categories** — tap a chip like **Favourites** or **Sparklers** to show only those items.
   - **Quick item** — for something not in your items (loose sugar, a one-off sale): tap the
     **Quick item** chip, enter the price, and optionally a name, quantity, unit and GST. It's added
     to this bill only, not to your item list. Searching for a name that doesn't exist also offers
     **Sell "…" once (quick item)**.

3. **Loose items or big quantities** — **press and hold** an item and type the quantity, for
   example **1.25 kg** or **24** boxes. For loose items already in the bill, tap the quantity
   (like **1.5 kg**) to change it, or tap **Remove from bill**.

4. When everything is added, tap **Charge ₹…** at the bottom to take payment — see
   [Take payment](take-payment.mdx). To check the bill first or give a discount, tap **Review bill**
   — see [Review the bill](review-bill.mdx).

## Tips

- **Grid or list:** tap the list icon next to **New bill** to see items as a list — handy when you
  have many items or long names. Tap the grid icon to go back. Your phone remembers the choice.
- **Start over:** tap **Clear** next to **New bill** to empty the bill. Tap **Undo** if you didn't mean to.
- Items show their price after discount, with the MRP crossed out when there's an offer
  (for example ~~₹1,800~~ **80% off ₹360**).
- An item that isn't in your list? Search for it and tap **Add "…" as new item** to save it, or sell it
  once as a quick item. Quick items count in sales reports, but not in profit (they have no cost price).
