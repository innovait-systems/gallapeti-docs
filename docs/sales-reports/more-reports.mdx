# More reports and GST

**When you'd use this:** at the end of the month or quarter, to see which items made money, and to
give your accountant the figures for GST filing. *Part of
[Galla Peti Pro](../settings/galla-peti-pro.mdx).*

## Open it

**Reports → Open more reports** (at the bottom of the Reports screen).

Pick the period at the top: **This month**, **Last month**, **This quarter**, **Last quarter**,
**This year (FY)** — or **Choose month** for any of the last 12 months. Quarters follow GST:
Apr–Jun, Jul–Sep, Oct–Dec, Jan–Mar. The financial year runs April to March.

## Summary

Sales after returns, number of bills, profit, discounts, GST collected, and cash / UPI / card after
refunds.

<Screenshot src="sales-reports/more-reports-sum.webp" alt="More reports, Summary for last month: sales after returns, profit, discounts, GST and payments by cash, UPI and card." />

## Items

Every item sold in the period with quantity, sales and profit:
- **Top sales**, **Top profit** or **Most sold** order.
- **Profit and margin** show for items with a [cost price](../items/prices-and-discounts.mdx).
- Returned quantities are taken off.
- **Didn't sell** lists items in your list with no sales in the period, most stock first — good for
  spotting stock that's stuck.

<Screenshot src="sales-reports/more-reports-items.webp" alt="More reports, Items: top sales with quantity, sales, profit and margin for each item." />

## GST

Shown when your shop has a GSTIN in [Shop details](../settings/shop-details.mdx):
- **For GSTR-3B (3.1 a)** — taxable value, IGST, CGST and SGST for the period.
- **By GST rate** — taxable value and GST at each rate.
- **For GSTR-1** — B2B invoices (bills with the buyer's GSTIN), B2C invoices, credit notes, and the
  bill number range with void bills.
- A warning if some bills have items **without an HSN code**. Add HSN codes to those items so new
  bills carry them.

Credit notes count in the period they were made. Void bills aren't counted in sales or GST — they
only appear in the bill number range as cancelled.

<Screenshot src="sales-reports/more-reports-gst.webp" alt="More reports, GST: GST after credit notes, figures for GSTR-3B 3.1(a), and GST by rate." />

## Export to Excel

Tap **Export to Excel** and save the file to Google Drive, or send it to your accountant on
WhatsApp or email. It has these sheets:

| Sheet | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Summary** | Sales, profit, payments, GST by rate, B2B / B2C / credit note totals |
| **Sales register** | Every bill, credit note and void bill — date, number, customer, GSTIN, payment, taxable value, CGST, SGST, IGST, total, who billed |
| **Items** | Item-wise quantity, sales, value before GST, profit and margin |
| **b2b**, **b2cs**, **cdnr**, **hsn(b2b)**, **hsn(b2c)**, **docs** | GST sheets with the same columns as the GST portal's GSTR-1 offline tool *(GST shops only)* |

:::note
Galla Peti prepares the figures; it doesn't file returns. Ask your accountant to check them before
filing. Sales to customers without a GSTIN are treated as sales within your state.
:::
