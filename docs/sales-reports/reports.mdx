# Sales & profit reports

**When you'd use this:** at the end of the day or month, to see how your shop is doing.

Open **Reports** from the Home screen (or **Sales → Sales & profit report**), then pick **Today**,
**This Week** or **This Month**.

<Screenshot alt="Reports: gold total sales card with change vs yesterday, profit and bills, and the bar chart." />

## What you see

- **Total sales** — with the change compared to the previous period: yesterday up to the same time,
  the previous 7 days, or the same days last month.
- **Profit** — sales before GST minus the cost of what you sold. Only items with a
  [cost price](../items/prices-and-discounts.mdx) count; the card says so if some items have none.
- **Bills** — how many, and the average bill.
- **Chart** — sales by hour (Today) or by day, with the profit part in teal.
- **Payments** — cash, UPI and card.
- **Top items** — your five best sellers.
- **Discounts given** and **GST collected**.

Void bills are not counted.

:::note[Several phones?]
Each phone keeps its own sales. Add up the reports from each phone for the shop total — combined
reports are planned for Galla Peti Pro.
:::
