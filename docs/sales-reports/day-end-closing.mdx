# Close the day

**When you'd use this:** at closing time (or the end of a shift), to check that the cash in the
drawer matches the day's bills. *Part of [Galla Peti Growth](../settings/galla-peti-pro.mdx).*

## Steps

1. Open **Sales** and tap **Close day** (next to **Report**).
2. Check the figures. They cover everything **since the last closing** — the first time, since
   midnight:
   - **Net sales** and number of bills, with returns taken off
   - **Cash**, **UPI** and **Card** (after refunds)
   - discounts given and GST collected

   <Screenshot src="sales-reports/close-day.webp" alt="Close the day: net sales since the last closing and payments by cash, UPI and card." />
3. **Cash in the drawer at the start** — the change you kept before the first bill. After the
   first closing, Galla Peti fills this in from what you left last time.
4. Galla Peti shows what **should be in the drawer**: starting cash + cash sales − cash refunds.
5. Count the cash and enter it in **Cash you counted**. You'll see **Cash matches**, **Short ₹…**
   or **Excess ₹…**.
6. **Leave in the drawer for tomorrow** — the change you keep for the next day (leave empty if you
   keep all of it).
7. Add a **note** if you like — for example *₹200 paid to the milk vendor from the drawer*.
8. Tap **Close the day** and confirm.

<Screenshot src="sales-reports/close-day-count.webp" alt="Cash drawer: starting cash plus cash sales, what should be in the drawer, cash counted showing Short ₹9, and Close the day." />

## The day-end summary

After closing, the summary opens:
- **Print** it on your bill printer — it has a signature line, handy if a helper closes the shop.
- **WhatsApp** it — for example to yourself, or to the owner when someone else closes.

Past closings are listed at the bottom of the **Close the day** screen. Tap one to see or print its
summary again.

## Tips

- **Two shifts?** Close at the end of each shift. Each closing starts where the last one ended.
- **Short every day?** Check for cash refunds or expenses paid from the drawer — write them in the
  note so the next count makes sense.
- With [staff PINs](../settings/staff-pins.mdx) on, the summary shows who closed the day, and you can
  choose who is allowed to close it.
