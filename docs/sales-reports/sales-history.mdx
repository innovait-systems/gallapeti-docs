# Past bills

**When you'd use this:** to find a bill, reprint it, resend it or check today's total.

1. Open the **Sales** tab. The card at the top shows **today's sales**, number of bills, average bill,
   and cash vs UPI.
2. Bills are listed by day, newest first. **Search** by bill number, customer name or phone.
3. Tap a bill to see it exactly as printed, then **WhatsApp**, **SMS**, **Print** or **PDF** — or
   **Void bill** (see [Cancel a bill](../billing/void-bill.mdx)).

For totals over a week or month, tap **Sales & profit report** — see [Reports](reports.mdx).
