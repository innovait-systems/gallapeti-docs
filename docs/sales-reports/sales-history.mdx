# Past bills

**When you'd use this:** to find a bill, reprint it, resend it or check today's total.

1. Open the **Sales** tab. The card at the top shows **today's sales**, number of bills, average bill,
   cash vs UPI, and returns.
2. Bills are listed by day, newest first. **Search** by bill number, customer name or phone.
3. Tap a bill to see it exactly as printed, then **WhatsApp**, **SMS**, **Print** or **PDF** — or
   **Return items** (see [Returns](../billing/returns.mdx)) or **Void bill** (see
   [Cancel a bill](../billing/void-bill.mdx)).
4. **Bills | Quotes | Returns** at the top switches between bills, [quotes](../billing/quotes.mdx) and
   credit notes.

For totals over a week or month, tap **Report** — see [Reports](reports.mdx). At closing time, tap
**Close the day** — see [Close the day](day-end-closing.mdx).
